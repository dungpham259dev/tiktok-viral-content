# Why did these videos go viral?

Inputs per video: `meta.json` (views, likes, comments, shares, duration, caption) and `transcript.txt`, plus its row
in `viral.csv` (score, x = times the channel's usual, share lift, age).

## Per video

Write down, quoting the transcript (no paraphrase you cannot point to):

1. **Hook (first sentence or first 3 s)**: the question or claim, and whose problem it names (the viewer's wallet,
   a familiar place, a famous brand).
2. **Promise**: what the viewer will know by the end.
3. **Spine**: the 3-6 steps of the explanation, each with the number or example it uses.
4. **Turn**: the surprise or reversal, if any ("vì sao quán vắng vẫn sống" -> "they live on the lease, not the coffee").
5. **Ending**: answer, call to comment, or cliffhanger (part 1/2).
6. **Format**: duration, narrated vs talking head vs text-on-screen, series name.
7. **Why it beat its channel**: compare with the channel's usual subject; x and share lift say how much.

## Across videos

- Report a pattern only when 3+ videos from 2+ channels show it; say how many and which.
- Separate patterns of subject (what is talked about) from patterns of telling (hook, structure, length).
- Note what the losers of the same channels did differently when you have them.

## Output

A short formula: subject types that win, hook templates with real examples, structure, length, ending. Then a list
of remake angles for the user: same question, new script in their own voice, numbers re-checked at the source.
Never copy narration text, footage or music.
