# vn-economics

Vietnamese economics, business, personal finance and investing explainers on TikTok. Built 2026-11-10 from five
discovery runs; 117 strong candidate channels were screened by hand with an AI agent.

- `channels.csv`: 23 followed channels (tier `watch`) and 9 `maybe`. `median` = median views of their latest posts.
- `screened.csv`: every verdict. Discovery skips `no` channels; most were sellers, banks, courses, news outlets,
  gossip, politics, quotes or entertainment.
- `keywords.txt`, `tags.txt`, `queries.txt`: search terms ranked by how many good channels each found.
- `viral/2026-11-10.csv`: 74 videos from the last 30 days that beat their channel (score >= 5), tagged
  economics-business / personal-finance / investing. News, politics and tech posts were dropped by hand.
- `viral/2026-11-10-top20.csv`: 20 of them with distinct subjects.

What wins (2026-10/11): "mô hình X có ngon ăn không / lời ở đâu" (buffet, cyber game, quán nhậu, hồ câu, dạy lái xe),
"vì sao ... vẫn sống" (empty shops), hidden costs of daily life, and brand origin stories.
Refresh the viral list with `tiktok-viral viral vn-economics`; numbers here are a snapshot.
